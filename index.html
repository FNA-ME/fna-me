About

Farhana Nasrin Akter is pursuing a PhD in Mechanical Engineering at South Dakota Mines, with expected completion in December 2027. As a research assistant, she studies the synthesis, characterization, and performance of materials for lithium–sulfur batteries. Her background also includes university teaching, industrial procurement, and marine engineering.
